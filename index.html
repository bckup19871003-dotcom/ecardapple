Good questions.
Clear next steps.

From choosing a country to finding a missing email, the right next step should be easy to find.

Already bought a gift?

Choose the card for product guidance and the relevant seller’s help links. Keep the order reference from your receipt.

Where do I actually pay for my gift?

The purchase button opens the brand’s gift page or a selected seller such as CashStar, Recharge or Best Buy. That seller takes payment, sends the gift and handles the order. eGift Official Store is an independent discovery service; it does not collect payment or issue gift cards.

Is my gift sent instantly?

Digital does not always mean instant. Order review, payment confirmation and a scheduled send date can affect timing. Read the delivery note for your card and the seller’s final estimate before you pay.

Can I buy for someone in Canada?

Use the country selector for the recipient’s location. We show a Canadian purchase option where a suitable route is established. Some entries have only a US route here; ChatGPT’s current gift card is US-only. Steam has its own recipient and currency rules. Never assume a US card works in Canada.

Do Apple TV and iTunes have separate gift cards?

These are gifting use cases for the current Apple Gift Card. Check that Apple directly bills the intended service and that the card matches the recipient’s Apple Account country. A gift value does not guarantee a whole subscription period.

What if I entered the wrong email or picked the wrong card?

Contact the seller shown on your order confirmation promptly. Keep the gift unused while asking about possible changes. Digital gift cards often have restricted cancellation or refund terms; a correction or refund is not guaranteed. Card details include product-specific guidance.

I have a charge but no gift. Should I order again?

First check the receipt, scheduled delivery date, spam folder and the seller’s order status. A pending bank entry may not be a completed order. Ask the seller about the existing attempt before placing a second purchase.

Does a wallet label guarantee I can use it?

No. “Gift-card page” means there is a relevant published reference. “Seller help” is broader information. The seller’s current checkout decides what is available for your exact gift, device, account and location.

Someone asked me to buy a gift card and send the code.

Pause if someone demands gift cards for taxes, bills, account repairs or a refund. Keep codes private and contact the organisation independently. Read FTC gift-card guidance ↗
